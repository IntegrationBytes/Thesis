# Statistical summary

Honest-effort statistical reporting over the (track × prompt)
ablation cells from the main evaluation runs.

**Sample sizes (n = 100 vignettes per cell).**

**Reporting model.** All bootstrap CIs are 95% percentile
intervals over 10 000 resamples (seed fixed for reproducibility).
SHACL pass-rate CIs are exact Clopper–Pearson. Paired F1
comparisons report the mean within-vignette delta, its CI,
and an exact two-sided sign-test p-value. We do **not**
report Cliff's delta — at these cell sizes the paired
mean delta and its CI carry the same directional information
without an extra metric.

**F1 validity.** Triple-level F1 (precision/recall) is computed
in a canonical-IRI space established by `pipeline/iri_normalizer.py`:
Synthea-derived gold ABoxes use UUID-derived IRIs that the LLM cannot
reproduce verbatim, so the normaliser aligns LLM output IRIs to gold
IRIs via FHIR-grounded matching keys (rdfs:label, literal values, IRI
token overlap, structural anchoring). Without it, F1 would be ~0 by
construction; with it, every vignette contributes to the F1 mean.

**Output-presence filter.** Means for OC, PC, and SHACL pass-rate
are over vignettes where the system actually produced output
(``output_present=true``). Vignettes a system was never run on are
excluded — their absence is a missing-data problem, not a SHACL or
PC failure. Each system row prints its own present-count.

**PC interpretation.** Property Completeness measures, for each
typed instance the LLM produces, what fraction of its
SHACL-declared property slots are populated. PC = 1.0 means every
extracted entity has all its expected attributes; PC = 0.0 means
entities were typed but every property is missing. A graph with no
typed instances reports PC = NaN. PC complements SHACL conformance:
an empty graph passes SHACL trivially but scores no PC.

Generated by `pipeline/stats.py`. Raw output: `evaluation/outputs/stats.json`.

## Notes on interpretation

1. **Bootstrap CIs are percentile intervals.** The bound
   cannot fall outside the observed min/max without more data.
   A tight CI means the per-vignette scores agree, not that
   the estimate is precise in a population sense.

2. **Sign-test p-values are exact** (binomial against p=0.5)
   over within-vignette F1 differences, with ties dropped.
   Intermediate p-values mean the test cannot reject — not
   that the systems are equivalent.

3. **Clopper–Pearson on SHACL pass-rate** is the right CI
   for a binomial proportion. Pairs of intervals may overlap
   even when the underlying point estimates differ — those
   overlaps are directional evidence, not equivalence.

4. **Why no Cliff's delta.** The paired mean Δ F1 plus its
   bootstrap CI already discriminates direction; Cliff's δ
   adds a metric reviewers must learn without changing the
   conclusion at this sample size.
